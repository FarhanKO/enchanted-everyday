Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers Enchanted Everyday Uploader, a private tool operated by the owner of The Enchanted Everyday (theenchantedeveryday@gmail.com). It publishes videos to accounts that the operator owns on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

What the app accesses

  • The operator's own accounts only. The app signs in with OAuth to the operator's own channel, Page and profiles. It uses those permissions only to upload the operator's videos and to read their performance statistics.
  • No data about other people. The app does not collect, store or process personal data about viewers, followers or any other users. It stores only aggregate statistics (views, likes, comment counts, shares, saves, average view duration) for the operator's own posts.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

From YouTube, the app accesses only the operator's own channel: it uploads videos and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and it reads the channel's name and ID and its own videos' statistics (views, likes, comment counts, watch time). It stores the channel ID, the IDs of the videos it uploaded, and those aggregate statistics. It uses this data only to publish the operator's videos and to report their performance to the operator, and never shares it with anyone.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and devices

The app has no website or app for other people. It does not place cookies, and it does not store or read any information on anyone's browser or device.

Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored on a private computer or server controlled by the operator. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time. For YouTube, use the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; for the other platforms, use their connected-apps settings. Revoking access stops all further access immediately, and stored YouTube data is deleted within 7 days of revocation. To have all stored data deleted at any time, contact theenchantedeveryday@gmail.com.
