Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers Enchanted Everyday Uploader, a private tool operated by the owner of The Enchanted Everyday (theenchantedeveryday@gmail.com). It publishes videos to accounts that the operator owns on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

What the app accesses

  • The operator's own accounts only. The app signs in with OAuth to the operator's own channel, Page and profiles. It uses those permissions only to upload the operator's videos and to read their performance statistics.
  • No data about other people. The app does not collect, store or process personal data about viewers, followers or any other users. It stores only aggregate statistics (views, likes, comment counts, shares, saves, average view duration) for the operator's own posts.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored on a private computer or server controlled by the operator. Tokens are never shared with third parties and can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Deletion

To delete all stored data, contact theenchantedeveryday@gmail.com. Revoking the app's access on a platform stops all further access immediately.
