## Before a reference is approved

- Plain background, no text, labels, speech bubbles or panel numbers
- Wearing the exact `cast.toml` outfit, including the signature colour item
- Full-body front, three-quarter, side and **back** (backs and silhouettes are used a lot)
- Same face as the master portrait in every panel
- Drawn in the character half of the style block: clean ink outlines, soft watercolour washes
